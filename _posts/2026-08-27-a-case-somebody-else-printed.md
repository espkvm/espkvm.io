Two boards, a ribbon cable and two leads: on a desk ESP-KVM looks like a
project, not a device. People kept asking for a case.

Colin Hickey printed one for the Waveshare ESP32-P4-ETH with the C790 inside and
published the model:
**[ESP32-P4-ETH KVM Case](https://www.printables.com/model/1824874-esp32-p4-eth-kvm-case)**,
CC BY-NC. Both boards and the OTG cable to the target fit in it. HDMI, Ethernet
and that cable come out of one side, power out of the other.

He says the P4 board sits tight, so take care pulling it out again, and he would
like to hear how it fits. That, and other enclosures people are working on, is
in [the discussion it came from](https://github.com/orgs/espkvm/discussions/18).
