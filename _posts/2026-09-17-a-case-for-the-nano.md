The two printed cases so far were both for the ESP32-P4-ETH. Now the NANO has
one too.

[Crisspii](https://github.com/Crisspii) - who found and fixed a NANO problem
that went into 0.48.0 - designed a small box for the Waveshare ESP32-P4-NANO
with its ESP32-C6 and the C790 capture board, and published it on
[MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/de/models/3317162-esp32-p4-nano-c6-kvm-case).

![Inside the case: the NANO at the bottom, the C790 above it, and the ribbon between them.](/assets/blog/a-case-for-the-nano/inside.webp)

The two boards sit on top of each other, so the box stays small. Ethernet, USB
and HDMI come out of one end. The sides are vented, and there is room for a
10 x 10 x 7 mm heatsink if the chip runs warm. Five M2 x 8 mm pointed screws
close it, and the assembly notes are with the model.

There is no place for a status display yet. Crisspii plans a version with two
display options, and will also put the model on Printables.

All three cases are on the [hardware list](/#hardware), with the boards they fit.
