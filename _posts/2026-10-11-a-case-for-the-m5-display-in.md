The [Add-on Display In](/boards/m5-addon-display-in/) - the HDMI input for
M5Stack's [Unit PoE-P4](/boards/m5-poe-p4/) - comes as a bare board, with no
case. The Unit PoE-P4 has one, so the pair looked unfinished on the desk.

I drew a case for the add-on that matches the Unit PoE-P4 housing: same width,
same corners. The two sit together as one block.

![The other end: the add-on's USB-A port above the Unit PoE-P4's Ethernet and USB-C.](/assets/blog/a-case-for-the-m5-display-in/back.webp)

HDMI comes out of one end and the USB-A port out of the other, and there are
vent slots on top. Ethernet, USB-C and the side ports of the Unit PoE-P4 stay
free.

![The case on its own, upside down: one part, with four tabs along the edges.](/assets/blog/a-case-for-the-m5-display-in/part.webp)

It is one printed part and needs no screws: four tabs along its edges go into
the slots of the Unit PoE-P4 housing and hold it there. The tabs are thin, so
press it on gently.

The Add-on Display Out has the same outline, so the case
should fit it too; I have only tried it with the Display In.

It is the first printed case for an M5Stack board on the
[hardware list](/#hardware).
