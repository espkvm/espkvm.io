[@Pnwcomputers](https://github.com/Pnwcomputers) shared [a case](https://makerworld.com/models/3391060) for the Waveshare ESP32-P4-ETH and the Geekworm C790. It is the third one for this pair of boards, after [Colin Hickey's](/cases/p4eth-printables/) and [Fabrion365's](/cases/p4eth-makerworld/).

![The case from the HDMI end: the C790's HDMI port above the P4 board's USB-C.](/assets/blog/a-snap-in-case-for-the-p4-eth/case.webp)

Neither board needs screws. The P4 board slides in on rails and four clips hold it. The C790 snaps onto ledges above it, with stops behind it, so pushing in an HDMI cable does not push the board back.

HDMI and the P4's USB-C come out of one end, Ethernet out of the other. Version 3.6 adds a cutout above the Ethernet port for a USB-C panel socket, for power. There are pinholes on both sides for the Reset and Boot buttons, which you press with a paperclip. The lid is held by four M3x10 screws in square nuts.

![The Ethernet end, with a pinhole for the buttons on the side.](/assets/blog/a-snap-in-case-for-the-p4-eth/ethernet.webp)

It is a remix of a case by Duglum. It prints in PETG at 0.2 mm and needs supports only under the port openings. The MakerWorld page has the assembly steps and the parts list. It is also listed on [the board's page](/boards/p4-eth/) with the other two. The discussion is [#83](https://github.com/orgs/espkvm/discussions/83).
