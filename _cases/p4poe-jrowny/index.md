Drawn by Jonathan Rowny for his p4kvm, where the TC358743 on the ESP32-P4 first worked. It holds the Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-POE-ETH and the Waveshare HDMI to CSI adapter (19137) on M2.5x6 standoffs. About 36 g of PLA and under an hour on a Bambu printer.

There is an [enhanced remix](/cases/p4poe-dangamboa/) with a microSD slot and holes for the power and reset wires.
