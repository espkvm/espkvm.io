A front panel for a 10-inch rack, by [Crisspii](https://github.com/Crisspii). It
takes the [ESP32-P4-NANO / C6 case](/cases/nano-c6-crisspii/) - one or two of
them, with or without the OLED - so the KVMs sit in the rack next to the
machines they look after.
