Two halves close around the Mini. The bottom has two countersunk holes for wood screws, so it hangs on a wall. The back is a little wider for the cables.

- The Mini version has a cut-out over the Ethernet port, so you can press the cable's release tab.
- The Mini W version is drawn from a Mini. The author has no Mini W yet.
- The halves take M2 screws: machine screws with threaded inserts, or 12 mm self-tapping screws. Assembly notes are on the model page.
