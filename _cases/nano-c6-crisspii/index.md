By [Crisspii](https://github.com/Crisspii), who also sent the NANO fix in 0.48.0.
Ethernet, USB and HDMI come out of one end, the sides are vented, and there is
room for a 10 x 10 x 7 mm heatsink. Five M2 x 8 mm pointed screws close it; the
assembly notes are with the model.

- a version with a status OLED fits the plain case's top, so a printed case can be upgraded;
- a [10-inch rack mount](/cases/nano-rack-crisspii/) holds one or two of them.
