Every ESP32-P4 board

The ESP32-P4 boards I have looked at, and whether ESP-KVM runs on them. Supported boards are in the catalog; the others say what is missing.

What a board needs

  • 32 MB of PSRAM. Capture, the encoder and TLS do not fit in 16 MB. On the chip it reads NRW32 (rev 1.x) or NRW32X (rev 3.x); NRW16 boards do not qualify.
  • A free camera connector. The HDMI capture board plugs into the P4's MIPI-CSI: a Raspberry Pi style 15-pin or 22-pin ribbon. A camera soldered onto that connector, or a board-to-board plug, takes it away.
  • The P4's high-speed USB on a connector it can drive as a device. That port is the keyboard and mouse the target sees. Behind a hub, or not brought out at all, it cannot be.
  • A network. An Ethernet PHY on the board, or an ESP32-C6, C5 or C61 for Wi-Fi.

ESP32-P4 boards for ESP-KVM

Green has it, orange does not, no colour is not known yet. The four columns are the four needs above. For the camera, what counts is whether the capture board's Raspberry Pi ribbon fits: a 15-pin or 22-pin connector in Pi order. A connector of another kind, or with an unpublished pinout, stays unknown.

Boards for ESP-KVM terminal

ESP-KVM terminal is the other end: a board with a screen that shows a KVM's picture, with no browser. It needs a screen with touch and a network, but no camera connector and no USB to a target. Today it is written for the Tab5 only.
