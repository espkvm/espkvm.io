Hardware

  • Flashing replaces what is on a board. Wiring it to a computer’s front-panel header, power pins or USB ports is done at your own risk.
  • Some boards are configured from the vendor’s schematic and have never been run by anyone; they are marked “untested”. Pin maps, cable advice and measurements on this site come from the units I or other users had, and your board may differ.
  • The power and reset controls press real buttons on a real machine. A forced power-off can lose data on that machine.

Security

An IP-KVM gives whoever reaches it the keyboard, screen and power button of another computer. ESP-KVM has not had an independent security audit. Keep it on a network you trust or behind a VPN, change the default password, and keep it updated. Putting it on the open internet is your decision and your responsibility.
