## Which board

Take the [Unit PoE-P4](/boards/m5-poe-p4/). The [Unit PoE-P4X](/boards/m5-poe-p4x/) is the same board with a newer chip (rev 3.x). M5Stack does not sell it yet, and the two images do not swap: each one runs only on its own chip. The boot log says which chip you have (`chip revision: v1.x` or `v3.x`).

## What you need

The board and the [Add-on Display In](/boards/m5-addon-display-in/) make a working KVM: the add-on takes the HDMI picture and the board does the rest. The other parts here are optional: a status screen, a clock that keeps the time without a network, a relay for the target's power button, a button on the box.
